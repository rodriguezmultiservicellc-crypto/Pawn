  KEY DECISIONS LOCKED (Session 51):
    - A TENDER THAT MOVES SOMEONE'S BALANCE IS ONE TRANSACTION OR IT IS
      NOTHING. Store credit on a sale is an RPC, not three PostgREST
      calls, because three calls have three ways to half-apply.
    - CONSIGNMENT ACCOUNTING IS THE DATABASE'S JOB. Every payable row
      comes from a trigger or the payout RPC; staff RLS is SELECT-only.
      Money owed to someone who is not in the room is exactly where the
      app layer should not be the authority.
    - A SIGNED LEDGER, NOT EDITED ROWS. A reversal that lands after the
      consignor was paid leaves a NEGATIVE open balance and the next
      payout nets it off. Mutating the original accrual would silently
      erase a payment that actually happened.
    - REVERSALS CLAMP TO WHAT STILL STANDS. Found by the smoke test,
      fixed in 0055 before any shop had data.
    - THE FLOOR PRICE IS A CONTRACT. The trigger checks the effective
      per-unit price after the line discount, not unit_price — a
      discount must not walk through a promise made to the consignor.
    - NOTHING EXPIRES STORE CREDIT AUTOMATICALLY.
      settings.store_credit_expiry_days is what the shop TELLS the
      customer; unclaimed credit is a liability and several states
      treat it as unclaimed property. Wire an expiry job only after
      reading the statute.
    - TURNING A MODULE OFF NEVER HIDES AN OBLIGATION. Balances stay
      visible and consignment accruals keep firing when the gate is
      off; the gate only stops NEW activity.
    - COMMISSION IS A FRACTION EVERYWHERE (0.2 = 20%) — column, Zod
      schema and trigger all agree. The UI takes a percent and converts
      in a shared CommissionField, so "20" can never become 2000%.

--- Session 52 (2026-09-21) — Security: profiles privesc CLOSED + real browser smoke harness ---

 380. [CRITICAL] profiles PRIVILEGE ESCALATION — CONFIRMED LIVE
     VULNERABLE, THEN CLOSED (patches/0056). The scaffold hole flagged
     2026-07-03 and deferred four sessions was real here. Read-only
     audit against the live DB found `anon` AND `authenticated` holding
     table-level UPDATE on EVERY profiles column — role and tenant_id
     included — while policy profiles_update_own was
     FOR UPDATE USING (id = auth.uid()) with NO WITH CHECK, so Postgres
     reuses USING for the check and it pins row ownership only.
     ROOT CAUSE: RLS was being asked to do column authorization, which
     it cannot do. A row policy says WHICH ROWS you may touch, never
     WHICH COLUMNS. The grant layer is the column control, and Supabase's
     default grants had handed out everything.
     Exploit: PATCH /rest/v1/profiles?id=eq.<self> {"role":"superadmin"}.
     Payoff verified as total — my_is_superadmin() short-circuits every
     tenant policy, AND billing_invoices + tenant_subscriptions gate
     directly on profiles.role = 'superadmin'.

 381. THE FIX IS TWO LAYERS, NOT ONE (patches/0056).
     (a) GRANT layer — the actual control. REVOKE INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/
         TRUNCATE/REFERENCES on profiles FROM anon, authenticated; then
         GRANT UPDATE (full_name, avatar_url, language) TO authenticated.
         Column list chosen for THIS app, not copied from Tocoloro.
         `email` deliberately excluded: it mirrors auth.users.email and
         renders as identity in /audit and /team, so self-editing it lets
         one staffer impersonate another in the audit trail.
         created_at/updated_at excluded — a trigger writing a column does
         not require the CALLER to hold UPDATE on it.
     (b) Guard trigger trg_profiles_guard_privilege_columns — refuses any
         role/tenant_id change made under an end-user JWT. Exists because
         a lone REVOKE is one careless GRANT ALL (or one scaffold re-run)
         away from being undone. Escape hatch is a TRANSACTION-LOCAL GUC
         (app.profile_privilege_write) set only by
         claim_tenant_with_license_key, which 0056 re-creates for that
         purpose — that RPC is SECURITY DEFINER but is CALLED by the
         onboarding user, so their JWT is still on the connection.
     NO RLS POLICY WAS TOUCHED (CLAUDE.md rule 1).

 382. COMPANION SCAFFOLD HOLE — NOT APPLICABLE HERE. The
     schema_migrations-without-RLS hole does not exist in this project:
     there is no schema_migrations table (migrations run through
     scripts/apply-migration.mjs), and the audit confirmed every table in
     `public` already has RLS enabled. Also swept every other

 368. STORE CREDIT (patches/0052 + 0053). 0052 is enum-only —
     payment_method += 'store_credit' — because the Management API sends
     a patch as ONE transaction and Postgres refuses to use a new enum
     value in the transaction that adds it (same split as 0049/0050).
     0053 adds customers.store_credit_balance NUMERIC(18,4) maintained
     by an AFTER INSERT trigger on the new append-only
     store_credit_events ledger, with CHECK (>= 0) as the safety net and
     a per-kind sign CHECK so an app-side sign slip cannot corrupt a
     balance. Seven kinds: issue_return / issue_buy / issue_manual /
     redeem_pos / redeem_undo / clawback / adjust_manual. A partial
     unique index gives idempotency on the issue + undo kinds (a
     double-submitted return cannot credit twice; one redemption cannot
     be undone twice) while leaving redeem_pos and manual adjustments
     repeatable on purpose. settings.store_credit_enabled (off by
     default) + settings.store_credit_expiry_days (informational ONLY —
     nothing expires credit, it is a liability and several states treat
     unclaimed balances as unclaimed property).

 369. THE REDEEM RPC IS THE WHOLE POINT (0053). A store-credit tender is
     three writes that must be atomic: the ledger debit, the
     sale_payments row, and the sales.paid_total roll-up. Doing them as
     three PostgREST calls from a server action leaves three ways to
     half-apply a tender — credit taken with no payment recorded, or a
     payment recorded against credit nobody was charged for. So
     store_credit_redeem_on_sale(tenant, sale, amount, by) does all
     three under FOR UPDATE locks on the sale and the customer, and
     'store_credit' is deliberately EXCLUDED from paymentMethodSchema
     so the generic add-payment path cannot reach it. Companions:
     store_credit_undo_sale_redemption() (open sale — soft-deletes the
     payment, backs out paid_total, writes the compensating row) and
     store_credit_restore_for_sale() (void path — leaves the payment
     rows as history, exactly like the card-refund path, and puts the
     credit back). All three are service_role-only; the guards run
     first (Rule 10). Errors are raised as stable tokens
     (store_credit_insufficient_balance etc.) so the UI translates them
     instead of showing Postgres text.

 370. STORE-CREDIT SURFACES. POS: a tender block on the sale detail
     (balance, "use max" capped by BOTH the balance and what the sale
     still owes, per-redemption undo). Customer detail: balance +
     5-row ledger + an owner/manager adjust modal where direction is a
     CHOICE and a reason is mandatory (a clerk typing "-50" for "50"
     must not be able to hand out money). Returns: 'store_credit' as a
     refund method, offered only with an identified customer and the
     module on, checked BEFORE the return row is written. Buy-outright:
     'store_credit' as a payout method — issued AFTER compliance_log so
     a purchase that never reached the police-report ledger cannot
     leave credit behind; if the credit write fails the purchase STANDS
     (compliance_log is write-once by design, Rule 15) and the error
     tells staff to issue it by hand. Receipt PDF prints "Store credit /
     Crédito de tienda". The daily register correctly ignores
     store-credit payments — they are neither drawer cash nor a card
     batch.

 371. CONSIGNMENT (patches/0054). consignors are ALWAYS backed by a
     customers row (never a second copy of someone's identity), carry
     the DEFAULT commission + payout method, and get a per-tenant
     'CN-000001'. inventory_items gains consignor_id +
     consignment_commission_pct (FROZEN at intake — changing the
     consignor default never re-prices goods already on the floor) +
     consignment_min_price + consignment_expires_on, with a CHECK that
     a consignor implies source='consigned' AND a commission.
     consignment_payables is a SIGNED ledger, not a mutable row:
     kind='accrual' when a consigned line sells, kind='reversal' when
     the sale voids or the line comes back. Balance = SUM(payable_amount)
     WHERE status='open'. Negative is a VALID state — a reversal landing
     after the consignor was paid means they owe the shop, and the next
     payout nets it off; mutating the accrual instead would silently
     erase a payment that actually happened. consignment_payouts records
     each cash-out, and consignment_pay_out() settles every open row in
     one transaction (refusing a zero or negative net). Staff RLS on
     payables and payouts is SELECT-ONLY — every write is a trigger or
     that RPC.

 372. THE ACCOUNTING IS TRIGGER-OWNED (0054). accrue on sales.status →
     'completed' (covers retail completion AND layaway payoff, because
     both flip the sale to 'completed'); reverse in full on → 'voided';
     reverse pro-rata on return_items INSERT. Gross is sale_items
     .line_total — what the line actually realized after any LINE
     discount; a cart-level discount (where loyalty redemption also
     lands) is the shop's own giveaway and is NOT prorated onto
     consigned lines. A separate BEFORE trigger on sale_items refuses
     any line whose EFFECTIVE per-unit price (line_total / quantity,
     i.e. after the line discount) falls below consignment_min_price —
     guarding unit_price alone would let a discount walk straight
     through the floor promised to the consignor. Same-tenant guards on
     consignors.customer_id and inventory_items.consignor_id.

 373. [BUG] CONSIGNMENT REVERSALS DOUBLE-COUNTED (found by the smoke
     test, fixed in patches/0055 before any shop had data). Root cause:
     consignment_reverse_on_void() reversed the FULL accrual for every
     line on the sale and consignment_reverse_on_return() reversed its
     pro-rata share of the FULL accrual — neither looked at reversals
     that had already landed. So a line fully returned (−160) and then
     voided (−160 again) put the consignor at −160 on an item that only
     ever accrued +160, and voidSaleAction IS reachable on a
     'fully_returned' sale. Repeated partial returns summing past the
     quantity sold had the same shape. 0055 adds
     consignment_unreversed_fraction() and both triggers now clamp:
     return reverses MIN(pro-rata, remainder), void reverses exactly the
     remainder, either skips when the remainder is already zero.
     Verified: three 1-unit returns against a 2-unit line followed by a
     void leave the line at exactly 0.00, never negative.
